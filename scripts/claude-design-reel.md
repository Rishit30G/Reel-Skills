# Claude Design: Instagram reel scripts

Target length: 30-45 s. Talking head + screen recording of Claude Design.
Something new on screen every 2-4 s. The hook lands in the first 2 s.
CTA keyword: **DESIGN** (comment it, you DM the link).

## Script 1: "The reveal" (H1, #28) · ~35 s · best all-rounder

| Time | Say | On screen |

| 2-5 s | This is Claude Design, and once you try it, your regular design tool is going to feel ancient. | Text: **CLAUDE DESIGN** slams in (Rebound Out). |
| 5-9 s | You type one sentence. "A landing page for my coffee brand." | Typing the prompt, sped up. |
| 9-13 s | And it doesn't give you a template. It reads your brand first: your colors, your fonts, even your website. | Web-capture tool grabbing your site → color swatches pop in. |

| 18-23 s | Then export it to Canva, PDF, PowerPoint, or straight to code. | Four export icons pop in one by one (Quad Out). |
| 23-28 s | One company said pages that took them 20 prompts in other tools took 2 here. | Big "20 → 2". |
| 28-31 s | It came out in April, and Figma's stock dropped 7% that day. | Red stock arrow, quick. |

## Which to post first

Post **Script 1** as the main reel, and run **H2** and **H3** as trial reels with the same body.
Keep whichever hook holds the most viewers past 3 s. Once you've done your own test, Script 2
will likely beat all of them: a real result on screen is more convincing than a feature list.
